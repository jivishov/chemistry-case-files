Honors · error propagation

The balance is good to ±0.1 g and each volume read to ±0.1 mL, so the relative uncertainties add. Work out the density's relative uncertainty, then decide whether this piece can name its metal at all.

Smaller samples carry larger relative volume error, so their density is less certain — which is exactly when two neighbouring metals stop being distinguishable.

Accuracy is closeness to the true value. Precision is how tightly repeated trials agree. They are independent, and repeating a measurement only ever tells you about the second one.
