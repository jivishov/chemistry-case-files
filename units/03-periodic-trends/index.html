Honors · effective nuclear charge & anomalies

Why does the radius shrink and ionization energy climb across a period, even though every element keeps adding electrons? The valence electrons feel a stronger net pull. Core electrons shield the nucleus; the outer electrons barely shield each other.

Period 3Zeffradius (pm)first IE

As Zeff climbs from 1 (Na) to 8 (Ar), the radius falls and the first ionization energy rises. Same shells, stronger pull. This core model works best for the main-group elements.
